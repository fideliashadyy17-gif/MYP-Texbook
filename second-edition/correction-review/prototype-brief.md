## Inquiry sequence

1. **Engagement:** A workshop must check a triangular roof truss before cutting timber.
2. **Inquiry questions:** factual — what angle facts are given? conceptual — why must two shorter sides exceed the longest? debatable — when is a construction reliable enough to build from?
3. **Investigation:** test triples with compass arcs; record possible/impossible/borderline.
4. **Observations:** equality gives a straight line; less than gives a gap.
5. **Discovery:** state the triangle inequality only after testing.
6. **Conclusion:** justify with arcs and apply to a design constraint.

## Criteria and ATL

- Criterion A: angle facts, polygon sums, constructions.
- Criterion B: triangle-inequality investigation.
- Criterion C: reasons, diagrams, construction card.
- Criterion D: roof-truss/kite/path application with evaluation.
- ATL: “In order for a student to think creatively and critically, students must choose an appropriate geometric fact or construction, test a design and explain why the method is reliable.”
