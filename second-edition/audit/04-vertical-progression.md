# 04 · Vertical progression, gaps and duplication

Status: pre-rewrite. This analysis checks cognitive demand, not merely whether a topic name repeats. Page references are physical PDF pages. The framework treats topics as examples and schools as responsible for vertical articulation; the audit therefore proposes a defensible sequence for approval rather than declaring an official IB order.

## 1. Overall judgement

The five books contain a real progression in most core threads: number → proportion → algebraic structure; linear → quadratic → exponential/logarithmic models; angle facts → similarity/trigonometry → non-right trigonometry and proof; descriptive data → correlation → dispersion/regression; simple chance → compound/conditional probability. The main risks are not wholesale redundancy. They are:

1. MYP4–5 pathway ambiguity: several MYP5 chapters are extended/enrichment-leaning and need school approval.
2. Planner/assessment inflation: listing A–D does not prove the task provides evidence for all strands.
3. Local concept vocabulary and MYP4 map/opener mismatches.
4. A few confirmed mathematical/production defects that must be repaired before reuse.
5. Missing editable sources and full answer-key QA.

## 3. Confirmed duplication that is pedagogically acceptable

- Exponents/powers recur from MYP1 to MYP4 because the demand changes: notation → laws → exact/approximate bounds.
- Linear relationships recur from MYP2 to MYP4 because the demand changes: tables/graphs → gradient as rate → systems/models → method choice.
- Correlation appears in MYP3 and MYP5. Keep both if MYP5 adds regression, residuals, prediction limits and denominator/variance conventions.
- Feasible regions appear in MYP3 and MYP5. Keep both if MYP5 adds optimisation, integer constraints and sensitivity.
- Probability recurs from MYP2 to MYP5. Keep if sample-space reasoning becomes conditional/counting reasoning.

## 5. Confirmed defects that interrupt progression

- MYP2 p.136 and p.242 clipping: production repair before reuse.
- MYP3 p.189: “r≈0 rules out a linear trend” overstates evidence; replace with “suggests little linear association in these data”.
- MYP4 p.441: dice-game answer says a single game always has a winner/loser, but totals other than 7/8 award neither; probability of neither is 25/36.
- MYP5 p.145: SSA two-triangle condition is overgeneralised. For acute A with side a opposite A and adjacent side b, two triangles require `b sin A < a < b`; equality gives one, `a ≥ b` gives one, `a < b sin A` gives none.
- MYP5 capstone: matrix-order mismatch, day-origin ambiguity, profit-hint sign slip, open-top tank ambiguity, raw LaTeX, and Exercise 11B Q1(a) variable ambiguity. Answer-key errata do not repair the student prompts.

## 6. Progression rule for the second edition

For every revisited topic, write a one-line progression contract:

`MYPn does X with support Y; MYPn+1 adds Z; evidence of increased demand is task T.`

If the contract cannot be written, merge, move or remove therepetition. Do not remove a topic solely because its name appears at another level.
