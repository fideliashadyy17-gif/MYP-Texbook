# MYP2 chapter and subtopic audit

Status: pre-rewrite audit. First-edition PDFs are unchanged. Page references are physical PDF pages unless stated otherwise.

Year 2 bridge level. The book has 11 numbered chapters and 63 numbered subtopics. No official Year 2 objective table is prescribed in the consulted guide; the school must articulate Year 1→Year 3 progression. The audit therefore checks cognitive demand, not only topic presence.
