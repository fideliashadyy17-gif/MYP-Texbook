# MYP1 chapter and subtopic audit

Status: pre-rewrite audit. First-edition PDFs are unchanged. Page references are physical PDF pages unless stated otherwise.

Year 1 prototype level. The book has 10 numbered chapters, 45 numbered subtopics, and unnumbered investigations/applications/criterion tasks/reviews located in the feature register. Planner strands are recorded as printed; Year 1 Criterion C i–iv is consistent with the consulted guide, while task evidence should not claim a criterion merely because a planner lists it.

### MYP1 Chapter 1: Number systems and place value

- Pages: physical 18–34; printed 18–34.
- Framework branch: Numerical and abstract reasoning.
- Opener: key concept **FORM**; related concepts **QUANTITY · REPRESENTATION**; global context **ORIENTATION IN SPACE AND TIME**.
- Inquiry: How does a digit’s position change its value, and how can we describe that structure so precisely that comparisons and calculations become reliable?
- ATL focus: Critical thinking.
- Printed planner strands: A i,ii,iii; B i,ii,iii; C i,ii,iii,iv; D i,ii,iii,iv,v.
- Keep: Keep the place-value/zero explanation and multiple representations.

- Issues/limits: Planner lists all four criteria more broadly than the main task evidence supports.
- Decision for approval: Approve representation-focused prototype; confirm local entry fluency.
