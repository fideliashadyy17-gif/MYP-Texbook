## Internal arithmetic and reasoning notes (not a student answer key)

- **2.1:** Six factors of 2 give 64 (five multiplication signs); three factors of 6 give 216; four factors of 5 give 625. Gaps are 5, 7, 3. Seven groups of three and three groups of seven both give 21; three *factors* of seven give 343. Pairing eight factors of two yields four factors of four. The representations of 64 are 2⁶, 4³ and 8²: powers with base at least 9 and exponent at least 2 exceed 64; exponent at least 7 does so even at base 2. Six doublings give 64 and seven give 128, bracketing 81.
- **2.2:** Values are 10, 1,000, 1,000,000; 706,028 expands using exponents 5, 3, 1, 0 with coefficients 7, 6, 2, 8. The given expansions evaluate to 80,309 and 1,435. For 90,000 use 9 × 10⁴ and then 9 × 10⁶. The extremal permutation difference is 6,420 − 2,046 = 4,374. Incrementing 99,999 adds one to the ones and carries through five places, giving 10⁵.
- **2.3:** Squares: 64, 81, 100, 121; cubes: 8, 27, 64, 125. Roots: 12, 4, 1. Bound 78 strictly between 64 and 81. Removing two layers from a 5-edge cube leaves 75, with 25 per layer. The false rule gives 14 instead of 10. Of cubes 8, 27, 64 the first two are not squares; 64 is 8² and 4³. Square units and cubic units refer to different dimensions.
- **2.4:** First group: 44, 196, 96. Division/multiplication pair: 36 and 4. Subtraction/addition pair: 28 and 16. The two gaps can be (2, 7) or (3, 4). The intended bracket repair is (8 + 4) × 3² = 108; the competing expression gives 152. Discount bills: 1,850 FCFA and 1,350 FCFA. A bracket may group two, three or four adjacent terms (or a single term redundantly); the only outputs are 11, 13 and 19.
- **2.5:** Values 20 and 100; then 18 and 32; then 34 and 64, with difference 30 from two rectangles of area 15. Garden difference is (s+2)²−s², which is 20 at s=4; strips of 8 and 12 give the same result. Machine output is 46 at inputs 4 and −4. For positive n, divide by n to give n < 6, so n = 1–5. Successive border differences for n=2, 3, 4 are 5, 7, 9; at n=20 the difference is 41. The rule is 2n+1 from two strips and a corner. First ten positive odd numbers sum to 10², allowing the first term as the initial tile.
- **Archive and review:** Under the model, m images use m(s²+200) bytes. With 60 images there are 9,612,000 bytes if all small and 21,612,000 if all large; every upgrade adds 200,000. Compare 19,812,000 for 51 upgrades with 20,012,000 for 52. Test actual file bytes or investigate compression/metadata before relying on the model. Review values: 64 for all three regroupings; 200,503; roots 9–12 inclusive; 50 and 49; alternating additions total 78 (3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23).
